# Feature: 5a - Part 2 fields

**From build-plan:** feature 5a, the first of two sub-features of item 5 (Form part 2)
**Status:** built, awaiting the manual browser pass

## Goal

Build section 2 of the protocol, "Messdaten und Hydrologie": the six measurement readings taken at
the water, and the nine hydrology assessments of the stretch itself. Twenty-one fields, of which
sixteen are radio groups or checkboxes, a control type the form does not have yet.

Section 2 is a placeholder today. Feature 4b built part 1 out of dropdowns and text boxes, which is
all part 1 needs. Part 2 is where the protocol becomes a grid of choices, and parts 3 to 6 are more
of the same, so the radio group built here is copied roughly a hundred more times.

The rules that make hydrology appear and disappear are 5b. This sub-feature always shows every
field.

## Why this needs a step before the fields

The option labels do not exist anywhere in machine-readable form.

A PDF radio group stores only its export values. `hydrologie.breite` exports `1` to `7` plus `0`,
and the German words printed beside those buttons ("< 1 m", "< 2 m", and so on) live in the page's
drawing instructions, not in the field. Feature 4 hit this once already, for `gewaessertyp`, and
solved it by holding the eight labels in `backend/scripts/extract_form_definition.py` and pairing
them with the values read from the PDF. Part 2 needs the same treatment twelve times over.

So the first build step is backend, produces no visible change, and unblocks the three after it.

## Design reference

**There is no mockup for part 2, and no radio button anywhere in `prototypes/`.** The approved
mockups cover part 1, the submissions list and the reviewer view only. This is the one open
question in this spec and is called out again in the notes at the end.

Two references stand in:

1. **`prototypes/protokoll-teil-1.html` and `prototypes/mockup.css`** for everything that is not
   new: the section card, the legend, the 12 column grid, the label above the control, the hint
   below it, the `.callout` band. Part 2 must look like part 1 built it.
2. **Page 1 of `Resources/Fiaka_Resources/Formular_Protokoll_E-Befischung_V20260609.pdf`** for the
   arrangement. The legacy form lays each hydrology group out as one horizontal row of buttons
   under its label, which is what makes nine groups readable in the space of a page.

The radio group itself is therefore derived rather than copied. Concretely: MUI's `RadioGroup` and
`Radio`, laid out in a row that wraps, inside the existing `FeldRahmen` so the label, the hint and
the eventual error message behave exactly as they do on every other field. Colour comes from the
existing tokens in `frontend/src/styles/theme.css` and from `muiTheme.ts`. No new colour, no `sx`
override at a call site.

## In scope

- Twelve new option lists in `database/seed/form_version_20260609/optionslisten.json`, generated by
  the existing extraction script, covering the three Messdaten radio groups and the nine Hydrologie
  radio groups.
- `FeldRadio`, a radio group over a named option list, and `FeldHaken`, a single checkbox, both
  built on MUI.

  **Amended during the build.** The draft said both wear the existing field frame. `FeldHaken` does
  not, and should not: a checkbox is labelled beside the box rather than above it, and all four that
  exist sit inside a `FeldRadio`'s row, which already carries the frame, the hint and the error
  message for the group they qualify. Giving each its own frame would have put a label above an
  unlabelled box and broken the row. Recorded here rather than left as a silent divergence.
- The Messdaten block: `temperatur`, `leitfaehigkeit`, `sichttiefe`, `regenfaelle`, `truebung`,
  `schaumbildung`.
- The Hydrologie block: nine radio groups, the four "Ja" checkboxes that hang off three of them,
  and the two Schaetzwert text fields.
- `Antworten` grows a `hydrologie` group and six more keys under `messdaten`.
- German labels in `frontend/src/i18n/locales/de.json`.

## Out of scope

- **The two rules of part 2.** Hydrology suppressed for standing waters, and the Schaetzwert inside
  its band. Both are 5b, and until then every field is always shown and no value is checked.
- **Required-field enforcement.** Same as part 1: an asterisk marks what feature 11 will demand at
  submit, and nothing is blocked here.
- **`messdaten.uhrzeit`.** Already built in 4b and already rendered in section 1, following the
  mockup rather than the PDF's own filing. It is not moved.
- **Showing or hiding the "not applicable" value.** Every hydrology group exports a `0` that means
  "hydrology does not apply to this water", which the legacy form writes when the Gewaessertyp is
  standing. It is never offered as an option here. Writing it is 5b's job.
- **The backend half of anything.** No endpoint, no Pydantic model, unchanged from part 1 and for
  the same reason: features 2 and 3 are deferred.
- **`befischte_bereiche.ges_gew_breite` and `ufer_breite`**, which read like width fields but belong
  to the fished areas in part 5, feature 8.
- **`hydrologie_box`.** A read-only text field in the PDF covering the whole hydrology area, whose
  value is the sentence "Angaben zur Hydrologie sind bei stehenden Gewässern nicht relevant". It is
  not an answer and gets no key in the document; it is the message the legacy form shows in place of
  the section, so it belongs to 5b's suppression, not here.

## Build loop

Build one step at a time, never the whole feature at once.

1. Plan mode lays out the step before any code.
2. The AI implements just that step.
3. It shows the diff (not full files); you read it and understand it.
4. You approve, then choose whether to commit a checkpoint or roll straight on.
   Checkpoints are optional; `/complete` makes the real feature-level commit at the end.

Never accept a step you haven't read. If a diff is too big to review, the step was too big, so split it.

## Data / contracts

### What `Antworten` gains

Every value is a `string | undefined`, exactly as part 1, and every key is the legacy PDF path.

```
messdaten.temperatur          text, degrees C
messdaten.leitfaehigkeit      text, microsiemens per cm
messdaten.sichttiefe          text, cm
messdaten.regenfaelle         one of 1 2 3
messdaten.truebung            one of 1 2 3
messdaten.schaumbildung       one of 1 2 3

hydrologie.breite                 one of 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 (0)
hydrologie.breite_schaetzwert     text, m
hydrologie.tiefe                  one of 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 (0)
hydrologie.tiefe_schaetzwert      text, m
hydrologie.tiefenvarianz          one of 1 2 3 (0)
hydrologie.mit_flachstellen       "Ja" or absent
hydrologie.mit_gumpen             "Ja" or absent
hydrologie.linienfuehrung         one of 1 2 3 4 (0)
hydrologie.furkationen            "Ja" or absent
hydrologie.stroemung              one of 1 2 3 4 5 (0)
hydrologie.rueckstroemung         "Ja" or absent
hydrologie.fliessgeschwindigkeit  one of 1 2 3 4 5 6 (0)
hydrologie.wasserfuehrung         one of 1 2 3 4 (0)
hydrologie.stillwasserbereich     one of 1 2 3 4 5 (0)
hydrologie.gesamtprofil           one of 1 3 4 5 (0)
```

The stored value is the export code, never the German word, for the same reason the Anlass and the
Gewaessertyp store codes: that is what FiaKa receives today. The checkbox stores the string `"Ja"`,
which is the export value the PDF uses, rather than a boolean, so the document stays a direct match
and the store keeps its one value type.

**Three differences from the sketch in `project-overview.md`,** all resolved in favour of the legacy
path, which is the rule in `coding-standards.md`:

| Overview says | The PDF says | Taken |
|---|---|---|
| `messdaten.wassertemperatur` | `messdaten.temperatur` | `temperatur` |
| no checkboxes listed | `mit_flachstellen`, `mit_gumpen`, `furkationen`, `rueckstroemung` | all four included |
| `hydrologie.gesamtprofil` among nine | same, but exporting `1 3 4 5`, with no `2` | the values as exported |

The overview calls that block a sketch rather than a locked shape, so this is filling it in, not
overruling it. Worth a line in the overview at `/complete`.

### Undoing a radio answer

A dropdown has "Bitte waehlen" at the top, so part 1 can always be put back to no answer. A radio
group has no such thing: once a button is pressed, the standard control has no way back to nothing.
On a form filled in over several sittings that is a real problem, because a mis-clicked band cannot
be corrected to "not answered", only to a different wrong band, and it reaches FiaKa looking
deliberate.

`FeldRadio` therefore carries a small "Auswahl aufheben" action beside the group, shown only while
the group has an answer, which writes `''` the way clearing a text field does. `Antworten` already
distinguishes `undefined` (never touched) from `''` (touched and cleared), so nothing new is needed
in the store.

Two alternatives were considered and rejected:

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| A "keine Angabe" button inside each group | Invents a German label the printed form does not have, and it would be indistinguishable from the unlabelled `0` value the PDF really does export. |
| Clicking the selected radio again to clear it | Invisible, not keyboard reachable, and it breaks what a radio group is expected to do. |

This is a decision, not a given. It is cheap to flip before step 2 and expensive after, because
parts 3 to 6 copy whatever this does.

### How an option list is named

Twelve new entries join `ListenName` in `frontend/src/protokoll/optionen.ts`, each named for the
field it belongs to, matching the convention the existing lists already follow
(`ausruestung.egeraet`, `probestrecke.monitoringnummer`):

```
messdaten.regenfaelle      messdaten.truebung            messdaten.schaumbildung
hydrologie.breite          hydrologie.tiefe              hydrologie.tiefenvarianz
hydrologie.linienfuehrung  hydrologie.stroemung          hydrologie.fliessgeschwindigkeit
hydrologie.wasserfuehrung  hydrologie.stillwasserbereich hydrologie.gesamtprofil
```

Nothing about `Option` or `optionen()` changes. A radio group and a dropdown read the same list in
the same shape, which is what lets 5b or a later feature switch one to the other without touching
the data.

### What 5b will need from this

Named here so 5a does not paint 5b into a corner:

- **The band bounds.** The legacy form checks a Schaetzwert against the band chosen above it. The
  bounds are in the PDF's own JavaScript and are already known: width bands run 0-1, 1-2, 2-5, 5-15,
  15-50, 50-100 and 100 upward metres; depth bands run 0-0.1, 0.1-0.3, 0.3-0.5, 0.5-1, 1-2, 2-4 and
  4 upward metres. 5a must not reorder or renumber the options, because 5b keys its bounds off the
  export codes.
- **Whether the block renders at all.** 5b decides that from `probestrecke.gewaessertyp`. Keep the
  Hydrologie block one component with no internal knowledge of the type, so 5b adds a condition
  around it rather than threading a flag through fifteen fields.

## What the build found, and what it still owes

### Two documents were corrected, outside the planned scope

Reading the PDF for the labels turned up that **defect 7 in our own list was wrong**, so it is
withdrawn. It claimed a flowing water type hides the `>= 100 m` width band. The form hides fields
named `hydrologie.breite.7`, and those numbers are positions in the button list, not export values.
Every group's last position is its unlabelled `0`, and hiding "not applicable" for a flowing water
is correct. The band sits at position 6, untouched. Verified across all nine groups.

`docs/decisions.md` carried an open question resting on that claim, replaced with the real one.

The list had not been sent to FFS yet, so nothing has to be retracted with them.

### Still unproven, and why

Every automated check passes: `pytest` 24, `npm test` 63, `tsc`, `oxlint`, `ruff`, `mypy`, the
build, and the seed regenerating byte-identical. Two further checks were run by script: every option
list name and translation key the new components reference resolves, and each generated list was
read back against the printed page.

Playwright is not installed and `coding-standards.md` says not to add it mid-feature, so these
"done when" clauses in steps 2 to 4 have **not** been demonstrated and need a human pass:

- answers surviving a reload, in both blocks
- arrow keys moving within a group, Tab leaving it, and no keyboard trap across the nine groups
- each group announced with its own label, and each checkbox as belonging to the group above it
- nine stacked groups readable at desktop width and below 800px, without a horizontal scrollbar
- light and dark

`/try` gives the walkthrough. The build steps are ticked because the code is written, not because
these were seen working.

## Notes for the AI

- **Two things to settle before step 2**, both because parts 3 to 6 copy whatever step 2 does:
  1. **There is no mockup for part 2.** The radio row is being derived from part 1's tokens and the
     printed PDF layout. If a design for part 2 exists anywhere, ask for it and put it in
     `blueprint/reference/` before building.
  2. **How a radio answer is undone**, set out under Data and contracts above. The spec picks a
     small "Auswahl aufheben" action beside the group. Confirm or change it now.
- **MUI first.** `RadioGroup`, `Radio`, `FormControlLabel` and `Checkbox`, themed in `muiTheme.ts`,
  not hand-built native inputs. This is the standing rule in `coding-standards.md`, and part 2 is
  where it matters most: sixteen controls here, roughly a hundred more in parts 3 to 6.
- **Reuse `FeldRahmen`.** Do not build a second field frame for radios. The label association, the
  required marker, the hint id and the error id are solved there, and 5b needs the error surface to
  work on these fields with no further wiring.
- **Do not scatter `sx` colours.** Theme MUI once. No hex anywhere.
- **The radio group's label is a group label,** not a field label pointing at one control. There is
  no single input for `htmlFor` to reach, so name the group by reference the way `FeldAuswahl`
  already does for MUI's Select, and give the group `role="radiogroup"` semantics through MUI rather
  than by hand.
- **Never block saving.** `useAutoSave` writes whatever is in the form. Unchanged.
- **Do not add a dependency.** MUI already ships everything this needs.
- **Regenerate, never hand-edit, the seed files.** They carry a "generated file" note for a reason,
  and the backend will seed `FormVersion` from the same JSON.
- **Three questions were raised during the build. Two are settled, one is still open.**
  1. **Settled 2026-09-02.** The unlabelled `0` in each hydrology group means "hydrology does not
     apply". 5b writes it when the Gewaessertyp is a standing water, matching what the legacy form
     does today.
  2. **Still open, for FFS.** `gesamtprofil` exports `1`, `3`, `4` and `5` with no `2`, and prints
     them in the order 1, 4, 5, 3. Whether an option was dropped from the printed form at some point
     is unanswerable from the file alone. Not blocking: the four options that exist are transcribed
     as printed, and a fifth would be an addition rather than a correction.
  3. **Settled 2026-09-02.** "vor der Untersuchnung" on the middle Regenfaelle button is a typo in
     the source and stays corrected to "vor der Untersuchung". The rule this sets, for the roughly
     hundred more labels in parts 3 to 6: a display label's obvious typo is fixed on the way in, and
     a stored export value is never touched.
- The questions still open from 4b and 4c are unchanged: `bearbeiter.ort` is missing from the legacy
  form, `z.quelle` and `z.ps_nummer` may not be the surveyor's to fill in, the time picker rounds to
  five minute steps, the coordinate bounds are derived rather than given, and a Vorfluter chain ends
  at any name ending in "Rhein" or "Donau".
